# Not for sale

Kaspa sells nothing. This repository sells nothing. There is no vendor and no contract.

Kaspa is a proof-of-work network. Miners make the blocks. A valid transaction is included because a miner includes it. The blocks continue until the miners stop. A government can order SpaceX or Tesla to act. It can order a bank, a cloud, or an app store to cut a company off. It cannot phone a Kaspa office and have the chain switched off. There is no such office.

The tools are public. Anyone may build on them, for their own reasons, including people at SpaceX, Tesla, and the companies around them. Nobody has to.

These notes ask three questions of each branch:

1. **Exposure.** A government, or a company a government can compel, can already interrupt this work.
2. **Worth exploring.** Nothing is on fire. A public rail would still remove a switch that only one company holds.
3. **While miners mine.** What keeps moving after that switch is thrown, and what does not.

SpaceX and Tesla remain under the laws of the places they operate. This rail does not lift a spectrum license, a sanction, an export rule, or an order served on the company. It can keep a hash ordered, a covenant closed, and a KAS payment settled, with no issuer who can freeze them.
