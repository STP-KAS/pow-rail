# Not for sale

Proof of work is a product. Miners make the blocks. Developers maintain the software. There is no vendor and no contract. Anyone may use the infrastructure. Nothing in this repository is for sale. A person or a company decides whether that use has any value.

It cannot phone a Kaspa office and have the chain switched off. There is no such office.

A government, a bank, a cloud, or an app store can cut a company off. The order lands where there is an office.

Go fuck yourself. That line is for anyone who means to interfere with SpaceX or Tesla and thinks this chain is a door they can shut by phone. Musk said the words on 29 November 2023 at the New York Times DealBook Summit, to advertisers using money as leverage, with Disney's Bob Iger in mind. The record is [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/story/elon-musk-x-advertisers-interview/) and [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/2023/11/29/23981928/elon-musk-ad-boycott-go-fuck-yourself-destroy-x). The company can still be ordered. The office does not exist.

The company pages are below. The tools a page can put in sequence are a hash seal, a covenant, and a KAS payment. The reasons differ. Each page asks what a government can already stop, what is worth a look when nothing is on fire, and what still moves after the company switch.

SpaceX and Tesla remain under the laws of the places they operate. A license, a sanction, an export rule, or an order served on the company still binds them. What this rail can keep is a hash ordered, a covenant closed, and a KAS payment settled, with no issuer who can freeze them.
