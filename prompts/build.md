## What the landing page hosts

Proof of work. No product, no vendor, no contract. Miners make the blocks. A government order, a bank, a cloud, or an app store lands on the company, because that switch has an office. A hash can stay ordered, a covenant can stay closed, and a KAS payment can stay settled. A license, a beam, a contactor, and a vehicle command do not.
